IDIS GSX 2026 CAMERA FIX
========================

FASTEST GITHUB UPDATE
---------------------
Replace these three files in the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository:

  index.html
  styles.css
  ar.js

KEEP your existing compiled target file:

  assets/targets/gsx2026-two-sided.mind

Do not delete it.

WHY THIS FIX EXISTS
-------------------
MindAR creates the live camera <video> at runtime and appends it to the
A-Frame scene's parent. The previous page left the scene directly under BODY,
which made browser stacking/background behavior capable of hiding the live
video behind the WebGL layer.

This build puts both the camera video and A-Frame scene inside #ar-container,
forces the WebGL renderer alpha to transparent, and waits for a real camera
frame before declaring the camera live.

3. Hard refresh / close and reopen the tab.

5. Approve camera access.
6. You should see the live camera behind the scan ring.
